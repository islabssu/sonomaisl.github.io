Support the lab

Gifts to the Intelligent Systems Lab put undergraduates on research-grade robots. Every dollar is designated to the lab and spent on students and the hardware they work on.

Why it matters here

Undergraduate research in robotics is normally a graduate-school privilege. At Sonoma State it is not. Students in this lab run their own projects on six-axis research arms, present at conferences, and leave with work that would be unusual in a doctoral programme elsewhere.

That only holds while the hardware keeps up. Robot arms, GPUs, and sensors age out faster than a state budget cycle replaces them, and the difference between a student who trains a policy on a real arm and one who reads about it is almost entirely a question of equipment. Private giving is what closes that gap.

$100

Consumables and tooling

Grippers, fixtures, printed parts, and the cables and connectors that a working lab goes through continuously.

$500

A depth camera

Puts stereo vision on a station, which is the difference between an arm that executes a script and one that responds to what is in front of it.

$2,500

A semester of student research

Supports one undergraduate researcher for a term, so they can work in the lab instead of taking a second job.

$10,000

A complete station

A robot arm and the GPU workstation that drives it, which is the unit a student actually works at.

Where your gift goes. Gifts are processed by the Sonoma State University Foundation and designated to the Intelligent Systems Lab.
