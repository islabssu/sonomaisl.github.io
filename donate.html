Support the lab

Gifts to the Intelligent Systems Lab support hands-on research opportunities for undergraduate, graduate, and K-12 students.

Why it matters here

Undergraduate research in robotics is normally a graduate-school privilege. At Sonoma State it is not. Students in this lab run their own projects, present at conferences, and leave with work that would be unusual in a doctoral programme elsewhere.

That only holds while the lab hardware keeps up. Private giving is what closes the gap between the university's support cycle and the fast pace of change in robotics, AI, and sensing systems.

Example gift levels and what they support

$100

Consumables and tooling

The everyday supplies a working lab goes through continuously.

$500

Hardware and accessories

A depth camera, replacement servos, GPU boards, or robot kits.

$2,500

A semester of student research

Supports one undergraduate researcher for a term, including conference travel and publication costs.

$10,000

A complete platform

A complete station with a GPU workstation and robot arms, a new robot platform, or a state-of-the-art training machine.

Where your gift goes. Gifts are processed by the Sonoma State University Foundation and designated to the Intelligent Systems Lab. The SSU Foundation is a 501(c)(3) non-profit, and contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive an acknowledgement from the Foundation for your records.
