Imitation Learning for Bimanual Robot Coordination

Adapting the Stanford UMI demonstration-to-policy framework for dual RX150 robot arms.

← Back to Research

Adapted the Stanford UMI demonstration-to-policy framework for dual RX150 robot arms to train a policy for bimanual block handoff. Built a hardware translation layer converting predicted UMI-style actions into physical RX150 joint movements. Presented at SSU SD Expo 2026.

Students: Nicholas Frangione, Kai Nunnemaker
