Sim-to-Real Transfer Learning for Robotic Manipulation

A unified reinforcement learning control architecture operating in simulation and on physical hardware.

← Back to Research

A reinforcement learning pipeline for robotic manipulators with a unified control architecture operating in simulation and on physical hardware, incorporating vision-based detection and control, ROS, and NVIDIA GPU acceleration.

Findings from this project are integrated directly into the ISL robotics course, giving students hands-on exposure to the same sim-to-real pipeline used in active lab research.

Engineering intelligent systems for the real world.
