Gesture-Controlled Robotic Arm

A sensor-equipped glove that translates hand gestures into robot arm commands.

← Back to Research

Built a sensor-equipped glove that translated hand gestures into robot arm commands, enabling intuitive physical human-robot interaction. One of the lab's earliest robotics projects, it explored direct gesture-based teleoperation as an alternative to conventional joystick or programmed control.

Engineering intelligent systems for the real world.
