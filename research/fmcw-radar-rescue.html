Radar Remote Detection of Victims Trapped by Debris

A low-cost FMCW radar system for locating victims trapped under debris following earthquakes and other disasters.

← Back to Research

Developed a low-cost FMCW radar system for locating victims trapped under debris following earthquakes and other disasters. Demonstrated detection of small movements — such as breathing — through concrete.

Completed as Khuram Shehzad's MS project, Detecting Small Movements through Concrete using FMCW Radar for Search and Rescue Applications (December 2020), with contributions from Anthony Arjona on system integration and testing.
