Non-Invasive Blood Glucose Monitoring via Breath VOCs

Estimating blood glucose from breath VOC signatures using custom sensors and on-device machine learning.

← Back to Research

Uses volatile organic compound (VOC) signatures in breath, chemical sensors, and a custom handheld device to collect breath data from type 2 diabetes patients. Machine learning models trained on clinical data are deployed on-device to estimate blood glucose states without finger pricks.

This is the lab's longest-running research line, spanning multiple student projects: early microcontroller-based classification work, a wearable wristband sensor prototype, an STM32-based VOC profiler board, and, most recently, a pilot clinical study with real patient data. The work has resulted in a U.S. patent and a peer-reviewed publication in Biosensors (2025).
