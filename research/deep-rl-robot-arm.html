Deep Reinforcement Learning for Robot Arm Control

Training a 4-DOF robotic arm to reach arbitrary target positions, accelerated by human demonstration data.

← Back to Research

Trained a 4-DOF robotic arm to reach arbitrary target positions using a Dueling Double Deep Q-Network (D3QN), accelerated by human demonstration data. Validated on both the PhantomX Reactor and Dobot Magician arms.

The work began with Kirk Durand's MS thesis (May 2021), which established the simulation-accelerated D3QN approach. Roel Hernandez's MS project (May 2022) then built a hardware implementation, translating the trained policy onto physical arms and testing transfer between two different 4-DOF platforms. The combined findings were published at ICDT 2025.
